Seven steps · Use them in order
01
Stop and breathe first
Before anything else. Four counts in, hold four, out four. Three times minimum. You cannot think your way out of anxiety while your nervous system is still activated — the breath comes first.
The body leads. The mind follows.
02
Name what's happening in your body
Tap where you feel it. Your body is talking — let's listen.

Start from the centre. Work outward.
04
Ground yourself in the room
Five things you can see. Four you can touch. Three you can hear. Two you can smell. One you can taste. Pulls you back into the present, out of the imagined future where the fear lives.
Anxiety lives in the future. You live here, now.
05
Say the fear out loud or write it down
One sentence. Gets it out of your head and into a form you can look at. Anxiety is loud in the dark and quiet in the light.
Name it. It gets smaller the moment you do.
06
Ask one question only
"Is there anything I can do about this right now, today?" If yes — do it. If no — you have permission to set it down. Not forever. Just for now.
If you can't control it, worrying is a tax on something you didn't buy.
07
Go on a quick 5 minute walk
Leave the headphones at home. No music, no podcast. Just you and the outside air. Anxiety is stored energy — walking moves it through you. The silence is part of the medicine.
Motion tells your nervous system the threat has passed.
08
Talk to someone
Not to solve it — just to say it out loud to another person. The spiral stays a spiral when it's only in your head. You don't have to carry this alone, Naoise.
You have someone who wants to hear from you.
